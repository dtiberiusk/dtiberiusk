Gauge Rules

  • 16 ga if: (i) two sides ≤ 22", or (ii) largest side = 31" and the other two ≤ 25".
  • 14 ga if: (i) two sides ≤ 33", or (ii) largest side = 47" and the other two ≤ 38".
  • 12 ga otherwise.

Split Cover Rules

  • 16 ga: Yes if W > 24 or H > 24 or W×H > 360.
  • 14 ga: Yes if W > 40 or H > 40 or W×H > 1000.
  • 12 ga: Yes if W > 60 or H > 60 or W×H > 1500.
